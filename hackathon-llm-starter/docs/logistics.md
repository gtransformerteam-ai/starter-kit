# Venue and Logistics

The hackathon takes place in Building B, Room 200. Doors open at 8:00 AM on Saturday.

Wi-Fi network: HackNet. The password is printed on the back of your badge.

Meals: breakfast at 8:30 AM, lunch at 12:30 PM, and dinner at 7:00 PM on Saturday. Breakfast and lunch are served on Sunday. Vegetarian and vegan options are available at every meal.

The building stays open overnight. Quiet rooms with couches are available on the third floor for anyone who needs to rest.

Parking is free in Lot C. Bring your badge to show security after 10:00 PM.
