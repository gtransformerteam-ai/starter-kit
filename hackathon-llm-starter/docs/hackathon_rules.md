# Hackathon Rules

Teams can have between 1 and 4 members. Every team member must be registered before the kickoff session.

All code must be written during the hackathon. You may use open-source libraries, public APIs, and starter kits like this one, but pre-built projects are not allowed.

Final submissions are due Sunday at 2:00 PM. Late submissions will not be judged.

Each team gets 3 minutes to demo, followed by 2 minutes of questions from the judges.

Projects are judged on four criteria, weighted equally: technical difficulty, creativity, usefulness, and quality of the demo.
